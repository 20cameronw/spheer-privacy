Spheer Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 21, 2026
Developer: Cameron Weston
Privacy contact: admin@glenpickle.com

This Privacy Policy explains how Cameron Weston ("we," "us," or "our") handles information in connection with Spheer.

Current version: Spheer stores gameplay information locally on your device. Advertising, online accounts, online leaderboards, cloud saves, developer gameplay analytics, and in-app purchases are currently disabled. Advertising software is included for future integration, but the game does not initialize it or request advertisements while advertising remains disabled.

1. Information stored on your device

Spheer stores information on your device to operate the game, including game progress, inventory, upgrades, settings, privacy preferences, and local save-recovery information. The game also includes local records for rewarded-ad limits, but rewarded ads are not currently available.

The current version does not automatically upload your game save or these preferences to servers operated by us.

Your device or platform provider may back up app data according to its own settings and policies. Deleting the app does not necessarily delete copies retained in platform backups.

2. Advertising

Advertisements are currently disabled for all players. The game does not currently request ads or grant rewards for watching ads.

Spheer includes Unity LevelPlay and Unity Ads software for a planned optional rewarded-ad feature. Including this software does not mean that advertising is enabled.

Before enabling advertising, we will update this policy to describe the information processed, advertising providers, purposes, retention practices, and privacy choices. We will also implement the audience-specific protections and obtain any consent required by applicable law.

A child's decision to watch an advertisement will not be treated as verifiable parental consent.

Information about Unity's general privacy practices is available at https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy . This link does not mean all practices described by Unity apply to the current version of Spheer.

3. Online services, analytics, and purchases

Online accounts, online leaderboards, cloud saves, developer gameplay analytics, and in-app purchases are currently disabled. Spheer does not currently create a player account, submit leaderboard scores, upload game saves, send developer gameplay analytics, or process in-app purchases through these features.

We will update this policy and provide any required notices and choices before enabling these features.

4. Information you provide when contacting us

If you email us, we receive your email address, message, and any attachments or other information you choose to provide. We use this information to respond to questions, provide support, investigate problems, and handle privacy requests. Our email service provider may process this correspondence to deliver and store it.

Please do not send passwords, payment information, government identification documents, or other unnecessary sensitive information. Parents and guardians should contact us on a child's behalf when possible.

5. How information is used and shared

Local game information is used to provide gameplay, save progress, restore local saves, and remember preferences. Support correspondence is used to handle the matter you contact us about.

We do not sell personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising in the current version.

We may disclose information we hold when reasonably necessary to comply with applicable law or a valid legal request, investigate fraud or abuse, or protect rights and safety.

6. Retention

Local game information remains on your device until removed through available game controls, app deletion, or device storage management. Device and platform backups may retain copies under their own policies.

We retain support correspondence only as reasonably necessary to respond, resolve the issue, maintain relevant records, and meet legal obligations. Retention depends on the nature of the request and any applicable legal requirements.

7. Your choices and privacy rights

You can:

  • Use the game's Reset Progress control to reset local gameplay progress. This is not a request to delete support correspondence or platform backups.
  • Delete the app and manage app data and backups through your device or platform settings.
  • Contact us about personal information we hold about you.

Depending on your location and the applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or obtain a copy of personal information, restrict or object to its use, or withdraw consent where processing relies on consent. These rights may be subject to legal exceptions.

To make a request, email admin@glenpickle.com. We may request proportionate information to verify your identity or authority. Please do not send sensitive verification documents unless we have agreed on an appropriate method.

Local game information is not automatically uploaded to us, so we generally cannot access or delete it remotely. We can explain the available device-local deletion controls.

Where applicable, you may appeal a privacy-request decision by contacting us or lodge a complaint with your local data-protection authority.

8. Children's privacy

Spheer is intended for a mixed audience, including children under 13. The current version allows children to play without creating an account, viewing advertisements, or uploading game progress.

Advertising and online services are currently disabled for all players. The current version does not offer targeted or personalized advertising.

We do not ask children to provide personal information to play. If a child contacts us directly, we may receive the information included in that communication. Please avoid sending a child's full name, address, school, photographs, or other unnecessary personal information.

If we learn that we have collected personal information from a child in circumstances requiring parental consent without obtaining that consent, we will take appropriate steps to delete it, subject to applicable law.

A parent or guardian may contact admin@glenpickle.com to ask about personal information we hold about their child, request its deletion, or request that we stop further collection or use. We may take reasonable steps to verify parental authority before responding.

Before introducing features that collect children's personal information, we will implement the protections required for those features, including verifiable parental consent where required, and update this policy. We will not condition a child's participation on providing more personal information than reasonably necessary.

9. International processing and security

If you contact us, your correspondence may be processed outside your country by the services used to deliver and store email. Those services operate under their own privacy practices and applicable legal requirements.

We take reasonable measures appropriate to the information we handle. No storage or transmission method is completely secure.

10. Policy hosting and third-party links

When you visit this policy webpage, its hosting provider may process technical information such as your IP address and request details to deliver and secure the website. This is separate from local gameplay data.

If this page is hosted on GitHub Pages, GitHub's privacy statement is available at GitHub General Privacy Statement .

External websites linked from this policy operate under their own privacy notices.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as Spheer changes. We will update the effective date and provide additional notice or obtain consent where required. This policy will be revised before advertising or online services are enabled.
